Project

Hasterza - A Path-traced C++ engine.

A compact C++23 engine with a Vulkan 1.3 hardware ray-traced renderer and a 6-DOF rigid-body physics engine. Geometry, lighting, shadows, reflections, and global illumination are all ray traced — this is where most of my engine-level curiosity goes.

About

H

Hi, I’m Haluk Oral.

C++ enthusiast, game engine developer, and proud father to six cats.

I’m a capable single-player gamer but, honestly, a terrible teammate. Some of my favorite games are the Fallout series, Prey, Kingdom Come: Deliverance I & II, Baldur’s Gate 3, and many others I’m surely forgetting. I’m also an avid science-fiction reader: Isaac Asimov’s works and the Dune series are among my favorites—and, of course, Arthur C. Clarke deserves a special mention. Also I love warm weather, and summer has always been my favorite time of year.

Throughout my career, I’ve worked with Unity, CryEngine, and Unreal Engine 3, 4, and 5. In recent years, while using Unreal Engine 5 professionally, I spent my free time building a game engine of my own. What began as a hobby has since grown into a passion. Today, I’m developing Hasterza: a C++23 and Vulkan-based engine built around modern rendering techniques, without a traditional rasterization pipeline. It is still very much a work in progress.
