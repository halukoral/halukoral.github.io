Project

Hasterza - A Path-traced C++ engine.

A compact C++23 engine with a Vulkan 1.3 hardware ray-traced renderer and a 6-DOF rigid-body physics engine. Geometry, lighting, shadows, reflections, and global illumination are all ray traced — this is where most of my engine-level curiosity goes.
